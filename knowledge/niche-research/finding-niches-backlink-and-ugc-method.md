## Smart work, not hard work

Think like the end user first: anyone building a real site also goes on to
build a Facebook page, a LinkedIn page, and brand it properly. That ordinary
behaviour is the shortcut in Method 1 below — a brand-new site betrays itself
by having only just linked to Facebook.[^iskills-course-notes]

## Method 1: mining a giant's newest backlinks

1. Open SEMrush and search **facebook.com**.
2. Go to **Backlinks**.
3. Check each linking site's **First Seen** and **Last Seen** dates — a site
   that both first and last linked to Facebook recently is a newly
   established site, not an old one that's been sitting there for years.
4. Apply a **link placement** filter, then export the newly-established
   sites specifically.
5. Hand the exported list to Claude (or another AI assistant) to analyze,
   then replicate whichever ones look promising.

This is a chore method in the sense that it takes going through a real list
rather than a single clever filter — but it's exactly the kind of list a
goldmine niche turns up in.[^iskills-course-notes]

## Method 2: UGC sites as an intent-mismatch detector

Reddit and Quora are useful here as a detector, not a destination: if either
one shows up ranking for a keyword, that keyword has an **intent
mismatch** — what's actually ranking (a discussion thread) isn't what the
searcher wanted (a direct answer or download) — and that gap is the
opportunity, the same intent-mismatch principle covered in *Niche research —
tools, metrics, and selection criteria*.[^iskills-course-notes]

To find these systematically:

1. Put a UGC site's domain (e.g. `reddit.com`) into SEMrush and open its
   **organic keywords**.
2. Apply an "include" filter — either a specific word (e.g. "download") or
   the 5W1H words (why, what, who, how, where).
3. Any keyword in the results where Reddit or Quora is genuinely ranking is a
   candidate intent mismatch.

**Worked example:** searching "Roblox Apk Download" and finding an Amazon
page or a Reddit discussion thread ranking for it, instead of a direct
download page, is an intent mismatch — and the gap it leaves is the
goldmine.[^iskills-course-notes]
