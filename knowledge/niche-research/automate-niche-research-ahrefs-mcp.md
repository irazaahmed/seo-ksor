Niche Research.*

## What you need

| Tool | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Ahrefs, paid plan | $129 |
| An AI assistant: ChatGPT Astra, Claude, Grok Bot or Muse | $20 |

Both costs are as given in class.[^seo-class-13]

## Steps

1. **Connect the Ahrefs MCP to Claude.**
2. **Start your research.**[^seo-class-13]

## How the research goes

- Once Ahrefs is connected, give Claude a prompt asking it to help you find
  keywords whose **DR is under 20** and that are **related to AI**.
- Claude does all the research itself and brings you keywords that will be
  **goldmines** for you.
- When you find a website you think you can replicate, have the same setup
  do its whole **reverse engineering**.[^seo-class-13]

## Getting a better answer

- The more refined the input you give, the more refined the answer you get.
- To fine-tune the input, you can take help from ChatGPT to write a good
  prompt.[^seo-class-13]
