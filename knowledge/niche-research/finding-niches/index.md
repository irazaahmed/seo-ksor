# Finding niches

* [Finding niches, Method 1: reverse-engineering marketplaces](finding-niches-marketplaces.md) - Finding a profitable niche by browsing site-selling marketplaces, reverse-engineering a successful site's keywords, and watching for brand hijacking.
* [Finding niches, Method 2: the Ahrefs/SEMrush filter method](finding-niches-ahrefs-filters.md) - Finding a low-competition niche directly inside a keyword-research tool, using volume, DR, and word-count filters.
* [Finding niches, Method 3: AI tools and NLP categories](finding-niches-ai-tools.md) - Using ChatGPT prompts and Google's NLP content categories to brainstorm low-competition niche and sub-niche ideas.
* [Finding niches, Method 4: backlink mining and UGC intent mismatches](finding-niches-backlink-and-ugc-method.md) - Mining a giant authority site's newest backlinks to find fresh niche sites worth replicating, and using UGC sites like Reddit and Quora to spot intent mismatches worth building for.
* [Finding niches, Method 5: SaaS and AI tool marketplaces](finding-niches-saas-and-ai-tools.md) - Reverse-engineering profitable SaaS, AI-tool, and app niches from marketplaces built for that stack: Acquire.com, Product Hunt, AppSumo, and the Chrome Web Store.
* [Finding niches, Method 6: replicating one strong page from Ahrefs Content Explorer](finding-niches-page-replication.md) - Using Ahrefs Content Explorer with a language filter and a low DR filter to find high-traffic pages, then building a dedicated website around one page rather than copying the whole site.
* [Finding niches, Method 7: a topic search in Ahrefs Content Explorer, then reverse engineering](finding-niches-content-explorer-topic-search.md) - Searching a topic "everywhere" in Ahrefs Content Explorer, filtering to Domain Rating 0 to 20, and reverse engineering the best site's organic keywords and its competitors to find goldmine niches.
* [Automating niche research: Ahrefs MCP connected to Claude](automate-niche-research-ahrefs-mcp.md) - Connecting the Ahrefs MCP server to Claude so that the assistant finds goldmine keywords (DR under 20, AI related) and reverse engineers a site you want to replicate, with a better prompt giving a better answer.
