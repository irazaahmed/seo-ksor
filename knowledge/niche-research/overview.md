Niche plays roughly a 70% role in whether a site succeeds or fails — get this
step wrong and no amount of later SEO work fully compensates.[^iskills-course-notes]

## Tools required

- **SEO Quake** — Chrome extension for on-page SEO data.
- **ChatGPT** — content ideas and keyword research.
- **Moz Pro** — Keyword Difficulty analysis.
- **SEMrush** — comprehensive SEO analysis.
- **Ahrefs** — keyword research, DR, and traffic analysis.
- **Moz Bar** — Chrome extension for SEO metrics.
- **Keyword Everywhere** — search volume analysis.
- **Similar Web** — website traffic insights.

## Metrics for SEO analysis

- **DA (Domain Authority)** — Moz metric for site authority.
- **PA (Page Authority)** — Moz metric for page-level authority.
- **DR (Domain Rating)** — Ahrefs metric for backlink profile strength.
- **Search Volume** — from Keyword Everywhere.
- **Keyword Difficulty (KD)** — checked via Moz.
- **Trend Analysis** — via Google Trends.

No single metrics provider is treated as ground truth — always sanity-check a
metric's authenticity against a second source before deciding on it.

## Monetization methods

- Google AdSense (earn through ads)
- Affiliate marketing (commissions on promoted products)
- Sponsored/guest posts (charge for link placement)
- Selling products/services (e-commerce, consulting, etc.)
- Subscription model (charge for premium content)

## Niche selection criteria

- Avoid single-word niches (e.g. Motivation, Beauty, Food, Samsung, Sports) —
  they are too broad to own.
- Focus on specific, problem-solving topics.
- Ensure at least 3 sites in the SERP have DA under 20.
- Prefer micro niches — Google favours them over macro niches.
- Ideal page count for an informational site: under 100–150 pages.
- Analyze only the first page of the SERP when judging competition.
- The lower the DR and the RD count of competitors, the easier the niche is to
  rank in.

## Types of niches, by size

1. **Macro niche** — e.g. sports, cricket.
2. **Micro niche** — e.g. world cup schedules.
3. **Nano niche** — e.g. a single country's world cup coverage.

Niches are also either **seasonal** or **evergreen** at each of these three
levels — a macro, micro, or nano niche can be either.

## Choosing a competitor

Pick at least one competitor to study, and choose a **weak** one: low DA, with
organic traffic you can plausibly replicate.

## Checking page count

Use `site:domain.com` in Google, or the site's sitemap, to see how many pages
a competitor runs — this is how the 100–150 page ideal above gets checked in
practice.
