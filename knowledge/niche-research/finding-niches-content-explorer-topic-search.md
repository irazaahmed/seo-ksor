*Taught by Sir Tanveer Nandla, in Class 12 of the SEO module, Niche Research
(Next Method).*

## Steps

1. Go to Ahrefs and open **Content Explorer**.
2. Type the name of any topic, for example **"AI convertor"**, and search it
   with **Everywhere** selected.[^seo-class-12]
3. Topics like this one can be pulled out of ChatGPT.[^seo-class-12]
4. Now play with the filters: set **Domain Rating** to **0 to 20**, apply it,
   and show the results.[^seo-class-12]
5. Click on the websites in the results. Each one shows its **total pages**,
   **total traffic** and **DR**.[^seo-class-12]
6. Take the website that looks best and go to its **organic keywords**.
7. **Reverse engineer** it. This is where goldmine niches turn
   up.[^seo-class-12]

## Go on to its competitors

When you explore any website in Ahrefs, it also shows that website's **top
competitors**. Open those as well, explore them and work on them the same
way.[^seo-class-12]

## Site age

A **younger** site is easy to compete with, and an **older** site is harder
to compete with.[^seo-class-12]
