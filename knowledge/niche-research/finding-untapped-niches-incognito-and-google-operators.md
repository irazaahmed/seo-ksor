*This whole document is from a make-up session of the SEO module, Niche
Research: Untapped Niches, taught by Sir Ghazanfar Abbas.*

This is one of the best methods of niche research, and it is the teacher's
own **tested** method. It reaches niches that **no tool** can tell
you.[^ghazanfar-abbas-untapped-niches]

## Before you start

- Turn on **incognito mode** in the browser.
- Log in to any tool there. Keep in mind that **no tool is exact**: they
  all give **estimated** results.
- The aim is to build a website on a niche whose **number of pages is
  small**.[^ghazanfar-abbas-untapped-niches]

## Part 1: an untapped keyword in a chosen country (the menu example)

1. First decide which **country** you will work in, and select that same
   country.
2. Google your target keyword, for example **"usa menu"**.
3. Now find a keyword whose name you have **never heard** before, which
   means it is untapped. Look for it in the **titles** of the pages in
   incognito mode. If you do not find one, go to the next page, until you
   reach a keyword you have never heard of.
4. When you find the keyword, check the traffic of **that website** with
   the **Ahrefs free traffic checker**. For example, if **greggs menu**
   shows up, look at the traffic of the first site that appears.
5. Then check that **keyword's** traffic with any tool.
6. Then look at the traffic of the keyword's **official or dedicated
   site**. In this example, search **greggs**, find the official site and
   look at its traffic. This gives you an idea of the potential.
7. After the official site, if there is a **local** site, look at its
   traffic too.
8. This **finalises** the keyword.[^ghazanfar-abbas-untapped-niches]

You can work the same way in **every language** and **every country** of
the world, not only in English.[^ghazanfar-abbas-untapped-niches]

## Part 2: an info niche in India, through government sites

1. To work in **India**, set the country to **India** and the language to
   **Hindi** in the settings.
2. There is a lot of work in India, and pages rank **very quickly**.
3. In India, most of the work is on **government** websites: their
   **schemes**, **portals** and **insurance schemes**. Look for those.
4. Type **`inurl: .gov`** in Google and explore. It means a URL that
   contains **.gov**.
5. The websites that come up get **a lot of traffic**. Open them and take
   ideas from them.
6. Put those websites into the **Ahrefs free traffic checker**. Their **top
   keywords** are written there too. Take those keywords and build websites
   on them. These are keywords that rank in **2 days**.
7. Build websites on keywords with **2.5 lac** (250,000) traffic a month,
   and your site gets **5 lac** (500,000) traffic, because there will be
   other keywords too and Google will give you
   traffic.[^ghazanfar-abbas-untapped-niches]

- Do not worry when you see a government site's **DR**: it is a government
  site. Look at **your own keywords**, and at how much traffic the **blogs**
  ranking for them get.
- You will find a great many untapped niches here.[^ghazanfar-abbas-untapped-niches]

## More search operators

- Search **`intitle: portal`** on Google the same way. Every title that
  says *portal* will come up.
- Instead of **`.gov`** in `inurl:`, you can use **`.org`**, **`.edu`** and
  many more, and find more untapped niches.[^ghazanfar-abbas-untapped-niches]

## Rules to remember

- Whenever you take an idea, check the traffic of the **domain**, not of
  the full URL.
- If the **official** site has traffic and there is **no local** site,
  consider that keyword: ranking there is **easy**.
- If your website ranks well, it also comes up in the **AI
  Overview**.[^ghazanfar-abbas-untapped-niches]
