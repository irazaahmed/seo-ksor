*Taught by Sir Naveed Kalro, in a short niche research session held before
Etsy Class 09.*

This is one more way to research a niche. Instead of looking for a whole
website to reverse-engineer, it looks for a single **page** that is already
pulling traffic, and builds a site around that page.[^naveed-kalro-content-explorer-session]

## Steps

1. Open **Content Explorer** in Ahrefs.
2. Apply a **language** filter only, for example **Portuguese**.
3. Apply a **DR** filter of **20**, so that pages from sites with a DR below
   20 come up.
4. Leave the search box **blank** and search.
5. Sort the results by **page traffic**, so the top pages come to the top.
6. Check the links **one by one**.[^naveed-kalro-content-explorer-session]

## What to do with a page you find

The page that shows up here is the one to target:

- **Replicate that page** and build a **dedicated website** around it.
- **Do not replicate the whole website.** The website may not meet your
  criteria, but this one page does, so the page is what you
  replicate.[^naveed-kalro-content-explorer-session]
