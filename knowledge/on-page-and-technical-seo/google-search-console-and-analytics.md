## Google Search Console

**Setup methods:** a domain property, or a URL-prefix property.

**Uses:**

- Track indexing status.
- Monitor search performance.
- Submit sitemaps.

## Google Analytics

Used to check user behaviour on the site, and to find traffic sources and
conversions.
