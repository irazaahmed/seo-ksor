## What are AEO and GEO?

**Goal:** get content ranked not only in Google, but also in AI platforms
and voice assistants.[^khurram-notes]

Target platforms:

- ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity
- Google AI Overview, Google Assistant, Siri, Alexa

> [!TIP]
> In future, a significant share of traffic will come from AI and voice
> search.

## Typed search vs voice search

| Old (typed search) | New (voice search) |
| --- | --- |
| `best dentist in Multan` | `Who is the best dentist in Multan?` |

So content should be **conversational and question-based**.[^khurram-notes]

## Where Google Assistant fetches its data from

1. **Featured snippets**: direct, concise (50 to 70 words), NLP optimized.
2. **People Also Ask (PAA)**: questions and answers are fetched directly.
3. **Schema markup**: business name, address, phone, services, FAQs,
   hours, coordinates.
4. **NLP content**: natural language that satisfies the user's intent, with
   no keyword stuffing.[^khurram-notes]

## The 13 AI ranking signals

Every signal matters, so do not miss any of them.[^khurram-notes]

1. **Natural tone**. Sources: YouTube transcripts, Reddit, Quora, Medium,
   PAA, Answer Socrates.
2. **Question-based content**, preferably as H2s. Sources: GSC, long-tail
   keywords, PAA, Reddit, Quora.
3. **Featured snippet optimization**: 50 to 70 words, direct, no fluff,
   conversational.
4. **Content structure**: headings, sub-headings, bullets, short
   paragraphs.
5. **Mobile optimization**: fully responsive, fast on mobile,
   touch-friendly.
6. **Natural content flow**: human-readable, natural transitions, no forced
   keywords.
7. **Site loading speed**: under 2 seconds; over 3 seconds is danger.
   Plugins: WP Rocket, LiteSpeed Cache, EWWW, Perfmatters.
8. **Local SEO signals**: citations, GMB, ZIP code, address, "near me",
   coordinates, hyper-local content.
9. **Semantic entities**: use words and concepts related to the topic
   naturally.
10. **Topical authority**: cover the main topic and all its sub-topics in
    complete depth.
11. **EEAT**: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness.
12. **Case studies**: real results, before and after, with numbers and
    data.
13. **Geo-tagged images**: geo-tag 40 to 50% of images, using the target
    city's latitude and longitude.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> "Do not miss any of these signals. Together, every signal improves the
> overall AI ranking." (Sir Khurram Malik)
