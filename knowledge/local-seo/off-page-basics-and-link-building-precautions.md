## What is off-page SEO?

Google does not only look at your website. It asks: **"Are other websites
giving you a vote of confidence?"** Off-page SEO is also known as off-site
SEO, link building, or backlink creation.[^khurram-notes]

## The 9 precautions (every one matters)

### 1. Low spam score (Moz)

| Range | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 10% | Safe, the ideal range |
| 10 to 30% | Acceptable, evaluate carefully |
| 30 to 60% | Risky, mostly avoid |
| 70%+ | A time bomb, never |

### 2. Bad neighborhoods

Gambling, casino, betting, adult, drugs, weapons: taking a link from these
damages your own site.[^khurram-notes]

### 3. Link farms

Sites built only to give backlinks, with zero real content. Risk of a
Google Penguin penalty.[^khurram-notes]

### 4. Auto-approval sites

Forum and blog comment links: fine if relevant, but avoid spammy
use.[^khurram-notes]

### 5. High-OBL sites

OBL = outbound links. On a page with 500 links, your link's value is
1/500.[^khurram-notes]

### 6. Paid / commercial sites

"1000 backlinks for $5" is 100% harmful. Evaluate quality
first.[^khurram-notes]

### 7. Penalized sites

A link from a Google-penalized site risks a negative association for your
site as well.[^khurram-notes]

### 8. Over-optimized anchor text

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **No exact-match keyword at all in your top 5 anchors.** This is Sir's
> most important point.

| Anchor type | Recommended share |
| --- | --- |
| Brand name | 40 to 50% |
| Naked URL (e.g. fiverr.com) | 20 to 30% |
| Generic (Read More, Learn More) | 15 to 20% |
| LSI / variations | 10 to 15% |
| Exact-match keyword | 5 to 10% maximum |

### 9. Same type of backlinks

Diversify: citations, foundational, social, Web 2.0, blog comments,
forums, niche edits, guest posts, podcasts, article
marketing.[^khurram-notes]

## Extra important points

- **Dofollow + nofollow mix**: a natural profile.
- **Link velocity**: gradual building earns Google's trust; a sudden spike
  is a Penguin flag.
- **Niche relevance**: same-niche links have the highest value.
- **Index your links**: an unindexed backlink has zero value, so get them
  indexed by Google.[^khurram-notes]
