*Chapter 6 of the Local SEO, AEO & GEO course notes, from Sir Khurram
Malik's client hunting lecture.*

## The core learning

| Don't | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Sell SEO | Sell **business results** |

Clients do not buy technical terms; they buy solutions to their
problems.[^khurram-notes]

## The wrong pitch

"Best Prices | Rankings | More Backlinks | Best SEO"

These are **features, not actual value**.[^khurram-notes]

## What brands actually sell: emotions

- Legacy, pride, status, experience
- Achievement, power, beauty, identity

People buy **desire, dreams, peace of mind, financial
freedom**.[^khurram-notes]

## The correct sales flow to show a client

> SEO → Ranking → Traffic → Leads → Customers → Revenue → Freedom → Peace
> of mind

> [!TIP]
> Pitch customers, revenue, freedom and peace of mind, not just SEO.

## What local businesses actually want

| Not this | This |
| --- | --- |
| Rankings, SEO, traffic | More phone calls, consistent and qualified |
| | Predictable income, stability every month |
| | Ad-free leads, customers without ads |
| | Calls during slow months |
| | Stable monthly income |
| | More free time and freedom to travel |
| | Less stress |

## Identity shift: who to become

- **"SEO expert"**: a technical service provider.
- **Business growth consultant**: a strategic partner, which means more
  value and higher fees.[^khurram-notes]

## Changing your language: 5 examples

| Technical language | Client language |
| --- | --- |
| "I create topical authority." | "I help your business become the obvious choice in Google searches for long-term visibility." |
| "I optimize Core Web Vitals." | "I remove technical bottlenecks that improve user experience and increase Google's trust." |
| "I build backlinks." | "I build authority that helps your business outrank competitors and attract more qualified customers." |
| "I'll improve your rankings." | "I help local businesses become the first company customers think of when they need your services." |
| "You spend thousands on ads. I'll stop that." | "Imagine getting consistent leads every week from Google, without paying for every single click." |

## Questions to ask in a client meeting

- What does your business actually do?
- What do you want to sell most?
- Who is your target customer?
- What are your business goals?
- Who are your biggest competitors?
- What would make this meeting successful for you?

> [!TIP]
> Understand the business before offering a solution, then pitch.

## What do we sell? Emotions

**1. FOMO (fear of missing out)**

- Your competitors are already getting more leads.
- They are ranking in AI Overview, and you are not.
- Potential customers go to a competitor every day.

**2. Hope**

- Growth, brand recognition, opportunities, long-term stability.

> [!CAUTION]
> Never use dishonest tactics: only real facts and genuine opportunities.

## Key takeaways

- **Sell outcomes**: revenue, freedom, peace of mind.
- **Sell emotions**: FOMO + hope.
- **Client language**: avoid technical terms.
- **Ask questions**: understand the business before pitching solutions.
- **Lead conversations**: you stay in control.
- **Be honest**: real facts, no dishonest tactics.
- **Identity**: become a business growth consultant.[^khurram-notes]
