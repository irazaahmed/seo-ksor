## Earning potential

If you follow the methods and practices Sir teaches properly, programmatic
SEO can generate a good monthly income.[^khurram-notes]

## Basic criteria

| Factor | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Population | Below 150,000 |
| Minimum payout | $70+ |
| Niche type | Micro / nano, not broad |
| Search volume | **Not** the primary factor |

## Personal branding

Alongside SEO, post regularly on Facebook and LinkedIn. Share your journey
and celebrate even small achievements. A dead profile means no
credibility.[^khurram-notes]

## Broad niche vs micro/nano niche

| Broad | Micro/nano (target this) |
| --- | --- |
| Plumbing | Leak detection |

**Why?** Google gets a clear topical relevance signal and the site looks
like a specialist: faster topical authority and less
competition.[^khurram-notes]

## The programmatic rank and rent workflow

1. **Select a vertical**: plumbing, HVAC, pest control, etc.
2. **LeadSmart coverage list**: select the vertical and export its cities.
3. **Remove duplicates** with a Duplicate Sentence Remover tool.
4. **Population filter**, with a ChatGPT prompt: "Separate cities with
   population below 150,000".
5. **Payout check**: confirm $70+ in LeadSmart.
6. **Final city selection**: population under 150K and payout $70+.
7. **City research** with ChatGPT: demographics, income, housing, age,
   local factors.
8. **Nearby locations** with ChatGPT: neighborhoods and nearby cities.
9. **Micro/nano niches**: "List micro/nano [vertical] niches based on
   demand + ranking visibility".
10. **Select one nano niche**: "I'm a beginner, select one nano niche for
    fastest ranking".
11. **Main keyword**: nano niche + city (e.g. "Las Vegas Leak Detection").
12. **SERP analysis**: are there specialist sites? Is the competition weak?
    Observe the SERP.
13. **State/location expansion**: nano niche, then state, then cities, then
    neighborhoods.
14. **Competitor and local research**: gaps, pain points, entities,
    questions.
15. **Website architecture**: prompt for the complete structure, with
    pages, neighborhoods and local issues.
16. **Tier-wise content plan**: Tier 1, then Tier 2, then Tier 3 (gradual
    publishing).
17. **Research-based content**: generate it section by section, not as
    blind AI output.
18. **Location pages**: nearby cities and neighborhoods.
19. **Phone number**: for a single city, a SignalWire local number; for a
    state or national site, a Ringba toll-free number.
20. **Programmatic expansion**: scale to more cities, states and
    locations.[^khurram-notes]

## The core formula

> - Right population (under 150K)
> - \+ Right payout ($70+)
> - \+ Micro/nano niche
> - \+ SERP opportunity
> - \+ Multiple locations
> - \+ Research-based content
> - **= a programmatic rank and rent opportunity**

> [!TIP]
> One nano niche + multiple locations = a scalable digital asset. Start
> small, expand systematically.
