## What SEO is

- SEO means **Search Engine Optimization**.
- A search engine is any place where we search and get results. Its
  optimization, its correction, is what we do, so that it gives us
  **authority** and **ranking**.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-seo]

## The Etsy ranking formula

> **Visibility + Clicks + Sales = Higher Ranking**

| Part | What drives it |
| --- | --- |
| Visibility | SEO |
| Click | CTR / thumbnail |
| Sale | Conversion / purchase |

Together these make a high ranking.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-seo]

- **SEO is not everything.** The real thing is **ranking**. Very often you
  put good keywords in and the video or post still does not go viral.
- Orders always come on the **first page**. We have to bring our product
  to the first page.
- If Etsy shows a product to **100 people** and **80** of them click one of
  them, that one ranks and its ranking goes up. This is called **CTR**.
- In the same way, if one listing gets **10 orders** and the others get
  **1** each, Etsy takes the one with more orders upwards.
- SEO ranks what people **buy** and takes it upwards. The most important
  thing is the **sale**.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-seo]

## Keyword layering

Select one keyword whose competition is low and that you can work on, such
as **"kaws wall art"**. Search it on **eRank**, and you will see different
keywords.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-seo]

The most important thing for SEO is **keyword layering**: write the
**low competition** keyword first, then the **mid competition** one, then
the **high competition** one.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-seo]

| Keyword | Competition |
| --- | --- |
| kaws wall art | 1,700 |
| kaws | 3,000 |
| wall art | 1 lac (100,000) |

Using these keywords, you move from **low competition** towards **high
competition**.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-seo]

## The parts of SEO

- **Title**
- **Description**
- **Tags**
- **Attributes**[^zakria-afzal-etsy-seo]

The **title** plays the main role in SEO. Write **high search volume**
keywords in it: if the title is good and has high search volume keywords,
the sale will come.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-seo]

## Building the title, tags and description

1. Search your **main keyword** on Etsy and add its **top 10 listings** to
   **Compare listing**.
2. Click **Show all tags** and copy all their tags. Give them to ChatGPT and
   tell it these are your competitors' tags.
3. Copy all their **titles** the same way, and tell it these are your
   competitors' titles.
4. Ask it for **13 tags** that the **majority** used, giving priority to the
   most used ones first, then the less used.
5. For the title, change nothing inside it: just write the keywords that are
   repeated most in the titles. One important thing: your own keyword (in
   this example **"kaws wall art"**) must be used, and at the **start** of
   the title.
6. Take the competitors' **descriptions** directly from Etsy, give them to
   ChatGPT, and ask it for a description for you, and to tell you where you
   have to make changes.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-seo]

Never use anything in the title that you are **not providing**.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-seo]

Now the title, tags and description for SEO are ready.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-seo]

## Why keep the same niche

- If we keep the niche the same, then even if we are not on the first page,
  our listing can show in the **related** fields of the listing that is on
  the first page. Through the tags, we can come in under the high listings.
- Whatever a person searches for, Etsy then shows them things related to it.
- So if someone does not like one of our listings, Etsy may show them our
  other listings because of **cookies**.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-seo]
