class of Sir Zakria Afzal's Etsy course on YouTube, a separate course from
the iSkills Etsy module. It follows [the practical niche and keyword
walkthrough](etsy-niche-and-keyword-research-practical.md).*

## The roadmap

1. **If you are still a beginner** and do not know a keyword or a niche,
   ask **ChatGPT**. It will give you a lot of keywords and seeds.
2. Put them into **eRank**, look at them step by step, and keep taking out
   the ones that meet the **criteria**.
3. Put each keyword you find into ChatGPT and ask whether it is
   **copyright** or **trademark**. If it is not, that is your
   keyword.[^zakria-afzal-keyword-roadmap]

## One keyword, one shop

Once you find **one good keyword**, build your **whole shop** on
it.[^zakria-afzal-keyword-roadmap]

## Getting more keywords out of a keyword

- You can take more keywords out of a keyword. For example, **wall art** is
  a keyword, and **wall** and **art** inside it are separate keywords.
- Just keep spying and reverse engineering, and you will find a lot of
  keywords. To sort them, you can use **Niche
  Desk**.[^zakria-afzal-keyword-roadmap]
