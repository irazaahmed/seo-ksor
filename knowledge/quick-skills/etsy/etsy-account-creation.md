## Start as a buyer account

Every Etsy shop starts life as a buyer account. Create it at etsy.com with a
**direct email address**, not "Continue with Google." Signing up with Google
here causes problems later in the seller setup, so email is the
requirement, not just a preference.

## Convert it to a seller account

Click **Sell on Etsy** to start the seller flow, and set:

- **Country:** Pakistan
- **Currency:** USD

If Etsy isn't available in your own country, select Pakistan anyway rather
than leaving this unset.

## Naming the shop

The shop name must be unique, and it should never be your own real name.
Keep it one of:

- niche-specific,
- general, or
- brand-specific.

## Skip the EtsyPlus upsell

Etsy offers an EtsyPlus subscription partway through setup; skip it.

## Secure the account

Turn on **"Keep your shop extra safe"** and authenticate it with the
**Google Authenticator** app, not SMS.
