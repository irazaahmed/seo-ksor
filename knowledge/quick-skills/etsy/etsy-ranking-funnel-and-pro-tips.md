## The Etsy ranking funnel

The secret of ranking taught in this class is the **Etsy ranking funnel**.
A buyer moves through it one step at a time:[^zakria-afzal-etsy-ranking]

1. **Impression**: the buyer sees your listing.
2. **Click**: the thumbnail and the title attract attention.
3. **Favorite / Add to Cart**: the buyer shows interest.
4. **Purchase**: the conversion happens.
5. **Positive review**
6. **Better ranking potential**

## Poor and healthy funnels

A funnel can be poor or healthy, and **SEO is not everything**:

- SEO only brings **impressions**.
- If impressions come but clicks do not, nobody buys.
- If clicks are not even **10%** of impressions, the funnel is poor.

So after SEO, clicks and the rest of the funnel matter for ranking
too.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-ranking]

## The thumbnail and first image

The first image should be so good that a buyer scrolling past **stops** on
it. The best image:

- **clearly shows the product**, so the buyer is not confused by the first
  image;
- looks professional;
- is readable on mobile;
- stands out from the competition;
- matches what the buyer expects.

A bad thumbnail has:

- a busy collage;
- an unclear product;
- weak contrast;
- too much text;
- text that is hard to read on mobile;
- no focal point.

A good thumbnail gets **more clicks**, a bad one
fewer.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-ranking]

## The listing image plan

The images in a listing should cover:

1. The main hero image
2. A close-up preview
3. The file format
4. Sizing and resolution
5. How the product is used
6. How to download
7. An important disclaimer
8. Support information[^zakria-afzal-etsy-ranking]

## Customer service and ranking

Customer service feeds ranking too:

- Reply to messages quickly.
- Maintain good reviews.
- Avoid cases.
- Deliver the correct files.
- Ship physical orders on time.
- Solve buyer problems professionally.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-ranking]

## Diagnosing a listing problem

| What you see | Where the problem is |
| --- | --- |
| No impressions | SEO |
| Impressions but no clicks | Thumbnail, price or title |
| Clicks but no sales | Product, trust or offer |
| Sales, then a drop | A seasonal product |

This is how the class diagnoses a listing problem.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-ranking]

## Pro tips

### Consistency, traffic and tracking

> **Consistency + Traffic + Tracking**

Publish **2 or 3 quality listings daily**, or 1 daily, or at minimum 1 every
2 to 3 days.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-ranking]

### The 30-listing checkpoint

After 30 listings, track:

- Which listings got impressions?
- Which got clicks?
- Which got favorites?
- Which listing brought an order?
- Which keyword brought the traffic?

As taught in the class, you cannot be successful **before 30 listings**, so
reaching this checkpoint is a must.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-ranking]

### Getting reviews

To get reviews, give **after-sales support**. Later, ask the buyer how they
found the product, and if they liked it, ask them for a
review.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-ranking]

### Bring relevant external traffic

Bring traffic from **Pinterest** as well, and keep your Pinterest posting
consistent.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-ranking]

### Never use fake orders

> Never use fake orders.[^zakria-afzal-etsy-ranking]
