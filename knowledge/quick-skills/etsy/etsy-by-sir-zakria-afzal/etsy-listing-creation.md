## Before you start: finalize your SEO

- Finalize the **title**, **description** and **tags** according to the
  guidance you have learned (see [Etsy SEO: the ranking formula, keyword
  layering, and writing the title, tags and
  description](etsy-seo-ranking-formula-and-keyword-layering.md)).
- Always put **your own keyword at the start of the title**.
- Take your **tags** from your competitors.[^zakria-afzal-listing-creation]

## The order to pick tags in

| Priority | Competition | Search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Low | High |
| 2 | High | High |
| 3 | High | Low |

Give priority to **low competition, high search** tags. Then use **high
competition, high search** tags, and after that **high competition, low
search** tags.[^zakria-afzal-listing-creation]

## How to create the listing

1. **Add photos.**
2. Put your **keywords in the image names** as well.
3. If you have made a **video**, upload it too. One video can be uploaded.
4. **Select the category**, for example **Digital Print**.
5. Use **Made on order** only when you are selling a **custom product**.
6. Now add the **title**, **description** and **tags**.
7. A **physical product** has material, sizing and so on. For a **digital
   product**, ignore all of these.
8. Set the **price** by looking at your competitors, but write the price
   **high**, then put a **discount** on it later, and keep adjusting the
   discount.
9. Look through the **remaining checks** and complete them as
   well.[^zakria-afzal-listing-creation]

## Create shop sections

Make **sections** in your shop as well, so that the buyer gets a good
experience.[^zakria-afzal-listing-creation]
