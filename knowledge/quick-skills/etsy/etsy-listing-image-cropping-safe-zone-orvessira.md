This is an **Orvessira-only production standard**, not an Etsy-required canvas, an Etsy-certified safe zone or a guarantee against every crop. The shop is [Orvessira](https://orvessira.etsy.com). Apply it to current and future Orvessira digital products; Wedding Planning & Stationery Templates is the current focus, not a permanent limit on the parent brand.[^orvessira-owner-standard]

## What Etsy says

Etsy recommends listing images at least **2000 px wide and 2000 px tall**. Its first-image minimum is **635 px in each dimension** to avoid lower search placement. Etsy recommends a landscape or square first image, a centered focal point and enough border for square, portrait and landscape thumbnail views. These are Etsy's recommendations; **3000 × 2400 px is Orvessira's own choice**.[^etsy-image-best-practices]

Etsy supports JPG and PNG, but says transparent PNGs and animated GIFs are unsupported as listing visuals; transparent areas may appear black. Etsy converts images to sRGB and advises converting before upload if colors appear wrong. Upload the final listing image as an **opaque** PNG or high-quality JPG in sRGB. Etsy may compress the upload.[^etsy-image-best-practices]

The first image should clearly and accurately represent what the buyer receives. For a visual digital download such as a printable or template, Etsy says to preview the actual content. Image-authenticity and AI rules can differ by item type, so check the current policy for the particular listing before publication. Do not use a mockup to imply that physical props or unlisted files are included.[^etsy-image-best-practices][^etsy-image-requirements]

Etsy's listing editor provides **Adjust Thumbnail** previews for its three thumbnail views. Check those previews before publishing; cropping a listing photo can change its thumbnail, and Etsy says a saved crop cannot be undone without re-uploading the original.[^etsy-edit-listing-photos]

## Orvessira canvas and zones

Measure coordinates from the **top-left** of a **3000 × 2400 px**, **5:4 landscape** canvas. These coordinates are owner-approved internal design rules, not Etsy requirements.[^orvessira-owner-standard]

| Area | X bounds (px) | Y bounds (px) | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critical hero zone | 720–2280 | 420–1980 | Central **1560 × 1560 px** for indispensable hero text, actual product screenshots, focal object and key visual information |
| Secondary content zone | 540–2460 | 300–2100 | Supporting details and gallery content; may be cropped in narrower thumbnails |
| General layout frame | 300–2700 | 240–2160 | Alignment and composition only; **not** a guaranteed crop-safe area |
| Outer edges | Outside the zones | Outside the zones | Nonessential decoration or background only |

Keep the core product preview large enough to read. If the hero becomes crowded, simplify it and move detail to other slides instead of shrinking the preview or text.[^orvessira-owner-standard]

### Six mathematical centered-crop checks

For a centered crop, use the full source height when the target ratio is narrower than 5:4; otherwise use the full source width. The table gives the source rectangle retained at each ratio. These **mathematical examples** informed the central safe zone; they do **not** map exactly to devices or Etsy placements.[^orvessira-owner-standard]

| Crop ratio | Retained X (px) | Retained Y (px) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1:1 square | 300–2700 | 0–2400 |
| 4:5 portrait | 540–2460 | 0–2400 |
| 3:4 portrait | 600–2400 | 0–2400 |
| 2:3 portrait | 700–2300 | 0–2400 |
| 4:3 landscape | 0–3000 | 75–2325 |
| 16:9 landscape | 0–3000 | approximately 356–2044 |

The critical zone lies inside these six centered source rectangles. Etsy may crop, zoom, recenter or overlay images differently. **No zero-cropping guarantee follows from this table.** The actual Etsy thumbnail adjustment previews remain a required check.[^orvessira-owner-standard][^etsy-image-best-practices]

## Orvessira visual identity

Use **ORVESSIRA** and the tagline **WHERE CREATIVITY COMES TO LIFE**. The approved palette is ivory `#FAF6EE`, sage `#879078`, dusty rose `#D99A8C`, champagne gold `#D8B887`, soft beige `#E8D8C5`, and supporting dark olive `#515A47` for readable text. Aim for an elegant, premium, warm, botanical, minimalist and professional look. Keep the parent brand flexible for future digital-product categories. These are Orvessira brand rules, **not universal Etsy SEO requirements**.[^orvessira-owner-standard]

## Listing image production workflow

1. Create a **3000 × 2400 px** canvas in Canva or another design tool.
2. Apply the appropriate Orvessira brand design system for the product.
3. Add the approved `Orvessira_Etsy_Transparent_SafeZone_Overlay_3000x2400.png` at full canvas size as a **temporary design guide**. If the asset is unavailable, use the exact coordinates above to construct a temporary guide.
4. Keep the truthful product screenshot or preview, focal point, indispensable headline and key visual information inside the critical hero zone. Avoid tiny text and cluttered collages.
5. Use separate gallery slides for detailed features, close-ups, real screenshots, file formats, sizing, how to use and download, disclaimers and support. Supporting details may use the secondary zone, but should remain legible in their intended view.
6. Remove or hide every guide overlay before export.
7. Export an **opaque**, high-quality PNG or JPG in **sRGB** and inspect the exported pixels for stray guides and legibility.
8. Test centered **1:1, 4:5, 3:4, 2:3, 4:3 and 16:9** crops. Check that essential hero content remains visible.
9. Upload to Etsy and inspect its **actual thumbnail adjustment previews**. Adjust position or revise the composition as needed before publication.
10. Verify desktop and mobile readability and every product claim against the files buyers actually receive. After publication, inspect live shop or search thumbnails where possible.[^orvessira-owner-standard][^etsy-edit-listing-photos]

Avoid misleading mockups, unverified product claims and images that imply nonexistent features, bonuses or physical goods. Check licensing and Etsy's current image-authenticity rules for the product type.[^orvessira-owner-standard][^etsy-image-requirements]
