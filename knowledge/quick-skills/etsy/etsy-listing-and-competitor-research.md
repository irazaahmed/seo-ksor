## Digital products, without investment

The main focus for a first Etsy shop, without needing capital up front, is
**digital products**.[^iskills-course-notes]

## Why most Etsy sellers fail

Most failures come down to one thing: the product display — the listing
itself — isn't right. Proper branding is what fixes it.[^iskills-course-notes]

- Keep the listing image built for a **mobile view** — most buyers browse on
  phones.
- Don't overload the listing image with text.

## Studying a competitor before you launch

Before creating a shop, read a competitor's shop in detail: figure out their
design, their products, their sale numbers, and the strategies behind
them.[^iskills-course-notes]

Pick the right shop to study or clone — not any shop:

- **Few listings** — a shop with a small catalogue is easier to
  reverse-engineer than a large one.
- **Under one year old** — an older, more established shop is a harder
  reference point to learn from at this stage.

Use tools for this research, but don't rely on them alone — manual review of
a shop still matters.[^iskills-course-notes]
